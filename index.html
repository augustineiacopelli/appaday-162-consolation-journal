    About

    This journal draws on the Rules for the Discernment of Spirits in the Spiritual Exercises of St. Ignatius of Loyola, chiefly the rules for the First Week. Rules 3 and 4 describe consolation and desolation, Rule 5 warns against changes made in desolation, Rules 6 to 8 counsel how to stand in desolation, and Rules 10 and 11 counsel how to receive consolation.

    Discernment is best done in company. If you are on retreat or making a serious choice, bring these notes to a spiritual director rather than reading them alone.
